 # 🚀 E-Commerce Marketing & Sales Analytics Project

This project is an end-to-end E-Commerce Analytics solution combining Python-based data processing and Machine Learning with a 6-page professional Power BI dashboard designed for executive and marketing decision-making.

The goal is to transform raw transactional and marketing data into structured insights, performance analysis, and predictive intelligence.

 ## 📊 Power BI – E-Commerce Marketing & Sales Performance Dashboard

A fully interactive Business Intelligence dashboard structured into 6 professional analytical pages:

 ### 🟢 Page 1: Executive Overview

Objective:
Provide a 30-second performance snapshot for leadership.

KPIs:

Total Revenue

Net Revenue (after discounts)

Number of Orders

Average Order Value (AOV)

Refund Rate

Total Customers

Visuals:

Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

Revenue by Channel

Revenue by Category

Top 5 Campaigns

Purpose:
Quickly identify revenue growth, dominant channels, and refund risks.

### 🟢 Page 2: Sales Performance Deep Dive

Objective:
Analyze product-level sales and pricing strategy impact.

KPIs:

Total Quantity Sold

Average Discount %

Premium vs Non-Premium Revenue %

Visuals:

Revenue by Category

Revenue by Brand

Revenue by Premium Flag

Discount % vs Revenue (Scatter Plot)

Top 10 Products

Business Questions Answered:

Which category drives the most revenue?

Do discounts increase performance?

Are premium products more profitable?

### 🟢 Page 3: Marketing Performance

Objective:
Measure campaign effectiveness and channel performance.

KPIs:

Revenue by Campaign

Average Revenue per Campaign

Expected vs Actual Uplift

Conversion Rate

ROI (when cost data is available)

Visuals:

Revenue by Channel

Campaign Performance Matrix

Expected vs Actual Uplift

Refund Rate by Channel

AOV by Channel

Strategic Focus:

Are paid channels outperforming organic?

Which campaigns underperform?

Did expected uplift targets materialize?

### 🟢 Page 4: Customer Insights

Objective:
Understand customer segmentation and revenue contribution.

KPIs:

Total Customers

Average Revenue per Customer

Repeat Customer Rate

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Visuals:

Revenue by Loyalty Tier

Revenue by Acquisition Channel

Revenue by Country (Map)

AOV by Gender

Customer Age Distribution

Insights Delivered:

Do high-tier customers drive most revenue?

Which acquisition channel attracts high-value customers?

Which markets are strongest?

### 🟢 Page 5: Customer Behavior & Funnel Analysis

#### Objective:
Analyze customer journey and optimize conversion performance.

#### KPIs:

Total Sessions

Conversion Rate

Average Session Duration

Conversion by Device

### Visuals:

Funnel (View → Add to Cart → Purchase)

Conversion Rate by Device

Session Duration vs Revenue

A/B Testing Results (Experiment Groups)

Strategic Outcome:

Identify drop-off stages

Compare device performance

Measure experiment impact

### 🛠 Technical Stack

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn)

Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

Revenue Prediction Model (Linear Regression)

Power BI (Interactive Dashboard Design)
